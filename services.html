Working together

Help writing your life story.

Everything you need to do this yourself is in the book, and I mean that literally — nothing is held back for a paid tier. But some people would rather not do it alone, and that is what this page is for.

What I do

I interview people about their lives, turn recordings into finished writing that still sounds like them, and get the result printed and into the hands of the family. Most of the work is the interviewing. The writing is the easier half.

If you already have the material — hours of audio, a folder of transcripts, a drawer of pages someone wrote years ago — I will shape that into the same finished book. Start with an assessment below.

Guided sessions

You do the writing; I keep you moving. Hour-long sessions, prompts chosen for your material, and honest feedback on what you produce between them. Four is the minimum; most people take four to six. Best if you like writing and want structure and a deadline.

$150a session · four minimum

Recorded interviews

I interview you — or a parent or grandparent — then deliver clean, organized transcripts along with the original audio. Six sessions is the minimum; eight to ten if a life has more in it. You can write from the transcripts yourself, or stop there. The audio alone is often what a family treasures most.

$300a session · six minimum

Revisions, and changes later on

A revision is an edit pass, not a rewrite. Corrections of fact, cuts, reordering, a lighter or heavier tone, line edits, and anything you remembered after reading the draft. New interviews or a chapter that needs fresh reporting is new work, and I will tell you that before I start it rather than after.

Two rounds, within ninety days of the first draft

Send your notes in one batch per round rather than a few at a time — it is the difference between a four-month project and a nine-month one. If several people are reading, one of you collects the notes and sends them to me. I would rather you argue with your brother than have me guess which of you is right.

Before you write to me

Two honest notes, so nobody wastes their time.

If your question is about craft, the answer is probably in the book. Stuck because everything reads flat? That is nearly always summary where a scene belongs, and Chapter 4 covers it. Can’t remember enough? Chapter 3. Started and stalled? You have likely taken on a scope you can’t finish, which is Chapter 2. I am glad to point you to the right chapter, and I do read every message.

If someone in your family is old or unwell, do not wait for me. Put your phone on the table this week and ask them one question. An imperfect recording made now is worth more than a perfect project that starts too late. That advice is free and it is the most important thing on this page.
