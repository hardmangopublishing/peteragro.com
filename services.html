Working together

Help writing your life story.

Everything you need to do this yourself is in the book, and I mean that literally — nothing is held back for a paid tier. But some people would rather not do it alone, and that is what this page is for.

What I do

I interview people about their lives, turn recordings into finished writing that still sounds like them, and get the result printed and into the hands of the family. Most of the work is the interviewing. The writing is the easier half.

Guided sessions

You do the writing; I keep you moving. Four hour-long sessions, prompts chosen for your material, and honest feedback on what you produce between them. Best if you like writing and want structure and a deadline.

Before you write to me

Two honest notes, so nobody wastes their time.

If your question is about craft, the answer is probably in the book. Stuck because everything reads flat? That is nearly always summary where a scene belongs, and Chapter 4 covers it. Can’t remember enough? Chapter 3. Started and stalled? You have likely taken on a scope you can’t finish, which is Chapter 2. I am glad to point you to the right chapter, and I do read every message.

If someone in your family is old or unwell, do not wait for me. Put your phone on the table this week and ask them one question. An imperfect recording made now is worth more than a perfect project that starts too late. That advice is free and it is the most important thing on this page.
